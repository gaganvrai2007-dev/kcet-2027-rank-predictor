How it works: the final KCET score gives 50% weight to your KCET PCM marks (out of 180) and 50% to your PUC PCM marks (out of 300). That composite is mapped to a rank using an approximate curve built for a typical year (about 2–2.5 lakh engineering rank holders). Real ranks shift every year with paper difficulty, number of candidates and tie-breaks, so treat this as a rough guide, not an official result. Check KEA’s official results for your actual rank.
